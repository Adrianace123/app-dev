### About Katekyo Hitman Reborn!
The story follows **Tsunayoshi Sawada** a clumsy and insecure middle school boy who discovers he is next in line to become the boss of the powerful **Vongola Famiglia** mafia organization. To prepare him for his future role the world's strongest hitman—an infant named **Reborn** becomes his home tutor turning Tsuna's everyday life into a chaotic series of battles and training sessions.
